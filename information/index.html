Housekeeping

Rooms are serviced daily between 9.00am and 3.00pm. If your room cannot be accessed during this time (for example, a Do Not Disturb sign is on the door), we will endeavour to service it later, subject to availability. If you would prefer your room serviced at a particular time, please let reception know in advance by dialling 0. Extra pillows, towels or toiletries are available on request.

Parking

Parking is free for hotel guests. Please register your vehicle registration number at reception when you arrive, as the car park is monitored and unregistered vehicles may receive a parking charge notice. Please park only in marked bays and keep the entrance and emergency routes clear.

Safety and good to know

In the event of the fire alarm sounding, leave the building by the nearest exit, do not use the lifts and go to the assembly point marked by the signpost in front of the hotel. Please do not re-enter until told it is safe to do so. All bedrooms and indoor areas are strictly non-smoking, including e-cigarettes; a deep-cleaning charge applies. In an emergency, dial 0 for reception, 24 hours a day.
