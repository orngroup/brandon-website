Leisure club and wellness

A few steps from your room: an indoor swimming pool beneath a glazed roof, sauna, steam room and a gym with cardio and resistance equipment. Use of the leisure club is complimentary for hotel guests.

Opening times
7.00am – 9.00pm daily (last entry 8.00pm)
  • Towels are provided at the leisure club; please leave your bathroom towels in your room.
  • Children under 16 must be supervised by an adult in the pool at all times.
  • Spa and beauty treatments can be arranged – please ask reception for availability and prices.
  • Seventeen acres of gardens and woodland are yours to explore – ask reception for the country walk map.

Housekeeping

Rooms are serviced daily between 9.00am and 12.00pm. If your room cannot be accessed during this time (for example, a Do Not Disturb sign is on the door), we will endeavour to service it later, subject to availability, as our housekeeping team's main operations finish at 3.00pm. If you would prefer a later service, please let reception know in advance by dialling 0. Extra pillows, towels or toiletries are available on request.

Parking

Parking is free for hotel guests. Please register your vehicle registration number at reception when you arrive, as the car park is monitored and unregistered vehicles may receive a parking charge notice. Please park only in marked bays and keep the entrance and emergency routes clear.

Safety and good to know

In the event of the fire alarm sounding, leave the building by the nearest exit, do not use the lifts and go to the assembly point in the front car park. Please do not re-enter until told it is safe to do so. All bedrooms and indoor areas are strictly non-smoking, including e-cigarettes; a deep-cleaning charge applies. In an emergency, dial 0 for reception, 24 hours a day.
