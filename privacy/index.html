Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa collects, why we collect it, how we look after it, and the choices you have.

Who we are What we collect How we use it Who we share it with How long we keep it Your rights

Who we are

Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa is the controller of your personal information. You can contact us at Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa, Main Street, Brandon, Wolston, Coventry CV8 3FW, by email at reservations@brandonhallhotelandspa.com or by phone on 024 7710 2555.

What we collect

When you stay with us

Your name, address, email address, phone number, arrival and departure dates, room and rate details, the names of people staying with you, any requests you make, and your payment card details for the booking and the security pre-authorisation. At check-in we may ask you to complete a registration card and show photo ID.

When you book a table or an event

Your name, contact details, date, time and number of guests, the occasion, and any dietary or access needs you choose to tell us about. We only use information about allergies or health so that we can look after you safely.

When you send an enquiry

When you use our event planner, contact form or email us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and the details of your event or stay.

When you apply for a job

Your name, contact details, the role you're interested in, your CV and anything else you choose to send us.

When you enter a competition

Your name, email address, phone number, your answers and whether you'd like to hear from us.

When you use our guest Wi-Fi

Your name, email address, the reason for your visit, and the date and time you connected.

How we use it

  • To manage your booking or event, including taking payment, holding a security pre-authorisation, and contacting you about your stay. This is needed to perform our contract with you.
  • To answer enquiries and prepare quotes. We rely on our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and on taking steps at your request before a contract.
  • To consider job applications, based on taking steps at your request before a possible contract of employment.
  • To run competitions and contact winners, based on our legitimate interest in running the competition you entered.
  • To provide guest Wi-Fi and keep it secure, based on our legitimate interest.
  • To send you news and offers by email, only if you've said yes. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as keeping accounting records and helping the authorities where the law requires.

We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who handle information on our behalf and only as we instruct:

  • Profitroom, our online room booking system.
  • ResDiary, our restaurant table booking system.
  • Google Cloud (Firebase), which hosts our enquiry, competition and guest Wi-Fi records. Only authorised members of our team can see them.
  • FormSubmit, which passes some website forms, such as job applications, to our team by email.
  • Our card payment provider, which processes payments and pre-authorisations securely.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contract clauses.

If you book through a travel agent or an online travel agency, they will share your booking details with us, and their own privacy policy also applies.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and payment records for as long as we need them for your stay and then for up to six years, to meet accounting and tax rules. Enquiries that don't lead to a booking are kept for up to two years. Job applications are kept for up to six months after the role is filled, unless you agree to us keeping them longer. Competition entries are kept until the competition and prize are complete. We delete information we no longer need.

Keeping it safe

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure systems, access only for team members who need it, and staff training.

Cookies

How this website uses cookies and similar technology is explained in our cookie policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, ask us to restrict it, or ask for a copy to give to another organisation. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email reservations@brandonhallhotelandspa.com and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
