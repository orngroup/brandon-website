Cookie policy

This policy explains how the Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa website uses cookies and similar technology, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. Websites can also store small amounts of information in your browser in other ways, such as local storage. In this policy we call all of these “cookies”.

Our approach

We keep cookies to a minimum. This website does not use advertising cookies, and we don't use cookies to track you around other websites.

Cookies we use

NamePurposeHow long
bhSplashRemembers that you've already seen the opening screen on your phone, so it isn't shown on every page.Until you close your browser
Event planner detailsRemembers the name and contact details you enter in our event planner, so you don't have to type them again.Until you clear your browser data
bh_comp_…Remembers that you've entered our current competition, so you aren't asked to enter twice.Until you clear your browser data

These are essential for the features you choose to use, so they don't need your consent.

Cookies set by other services

Some parts of our website use services from other companies, which may set their own cookies when you use them:

  • ResDiary, which runs the table booking form on our dining page.
  • Profitroom, which runs our room booking pages. These open on Profitroom's own website.
  • Google, for the map on our contact page and for the services that run our event planner and competition.

These companies' own cookie and privacy policies explain how they use cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking all cookies may stop some features from working, such as the table booking form or the event planner. The help pages for your browser explain how to do this.

More information

For more about how we handle your personal information, see our privacy policy. If you have any questions, email reservations@brandonhallhotelandspa.com.
