If you book on behalf of other people, you confirm that you have their agreement and that you will share these terms with everyone in your party. You are responsible for the booking, including any payments, charges and the conduct of your party.

Our team cannot change these terms unless we agree a change with you in writing. Where a specific offer, package, event contract or rate has its own conditions, those conditions apply as well, and take priority if they differ from these terms.

2. Your stay at a glance

Arrival and departure

Check-in from 3pm. Check-out by 11am on the day you leave.

A £50 card pre-authorisation is required at check-in (see section 4).

Children

Children aged 2 and under stay free when sharing existing beds. Guests aged 13 and over are charged at the adult rate.

Pets

Pets are welcome. Additional charges may apply, so please tell us when you book.

Parking

Parking is free for all hotel guests and you don't need to reserve a space.

If you'd like to arrive early or leave late, ask us in advance. We'll always try to help, but it depends on availability and a charge may apply. Rooms not vacated by 11am without an agreed late departure may be charged an additional night.

All guests must be able to show a valid photo ID and a credit or debit card at check-in. The lead guest must be 18 or over.

3. Room bookings and rates

Your booking

Your booking is confirmed when you receive a confirmation from us or from our online booking system. Please check the details carefully and let us know straight away if anything is wrong.

The conditions of the rate you choose, including when you pay, whether you can cancel or change it, and any deadline for doing so, are shown when you book and on your confirmation. They form part of these terms.

Non-refundable and advance purchase rates

These rates are paid in full when you book. They cannot be cancelled, refunded, changed or transferred to another person or date.

Flexible rates

Flexible rates are held with a valid card. You can cancel or amend free of charge up to the deadline shown on your confirmation. If you cancel after that deadline, or don't arrive, we will charge the first night of your stay at the booked rate.

If you leave earlier than booked, we may charge for the nights you booked but did not use.

Group bookings

Group, tour and corporate bookings have their own deposit, payment and cancellation terms. We'll set these out in your quote or contract.

Offers, packages and vouchers

Offers and packages are subject to availability and to their own conditions. They can't be combined with any other offer unless we say so, and they can't be exchanged for cash. Gift vouchers must be presented at the time of use and are valid until the expiry date shown.

4. Security deposit and damage liability policy

4.1 Pre-authorisation and security hold

The hotel reserves the right to place a temporary pre-authorisation hold of £50 per night on a valid credit or debit card upon check-in. This is a security measure intended to cover incidental expenses, outstanding charges, potential damage or additional cleaning costs.

The pre-authorisation is not an immediate payment. Where no additional charges are applicable, the hotel will release the hold upon check-out. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take approximately 3 to 7 business days, or longer in some cases, for the funds to become available.

4.2 Guest responsibility for damage and additional cleaning

Guests are responsible for damage, loss or excessive soiling caused to hotel property, rooms, furnishings or equipment by themselves, their visitors or animals under their supervision.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Damage to furniture, fixtures, fittings or equipment.
  • Stains, bodily fluids or other contamination requiring specialist cleaning.
  • Damage or additional cleaning resulting from pets.
  • Smoking or vaping in designated non-smoking areas.
  • Missing or damaged hotel property.

Normal wear and tear resulting from reasonable use is excluded.

The hotel reserves the right to recover reasonable and proportionate costs incurred for necessary repairs, specialist cleaning or replacement of damaged or missing items.

4.3 Charges for damage or additional cleaning

Where damage, loss or excessive cleaning requirements are identified, the hotel may recover reasonable, documented costs from the guest responsible.

Where permitted by the applicable payment authorisation and card provider rules, these charges may be applied against the pre-authorised amount or charged to the payment method provided.

If the costs exceed the pre-authorised amount, the guest remains responsible for settling the reasonable outstanding balance.

Where reasonably practicable, the hotel will provide details of the charges, including supporting evidence or an itemised breakdown. Guests may contact the hotel to raise any concerns or dispute a charge.

All charges will be applied fairly and in accordance with applicable UK consumer protection legislation.

5. Events, dining and Christmas

Weddings, meetings and private events

Private events, weddings, meetings and conferences are confirmed by a written contract, which sets out deposits, payment dates, final numbers and cancellation terms. Your contract takes priority over these general terms.

Ticketed and public events

Tickets for public events, including Christmas Party Nights, Joiner Party Nights, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve, are non-refundable and non-transferable unless the event's own terms say otherwise. Deposits secure your place and are not refundable if you cancel or your numbers go down. Event-specific terms are shown on the Christmas page and on your booking confirmation.

If we have to cancel an event, we'll offer you another date or a full refund of the money you paid us for that event.

Restaurant bookings

Please arrive at your booked time, as we can only hold tables for a short while. Please let us know if you're running late or need to cancel, so we can offer the table to someone else. Larger tables may be asked for a card guarantee or deposit.

Allergies and dietary needs

Please tell us about any allergy or intolerance when you book and again when you order. Our kitchen handles all 14 major allergens, so we can't guarantee that any dish is completely free from traces.

6. Prices and payment

All prices are in pounds sterling and include VAT at the current rate. If the rate of VAT changes, we may adjust any balance that hasn't yet been paid.

Prices shown on our website and in brochures can change. The price you pay is the one confirmed at the time of booking. We may review event and package prices for future dates, but we won't increase the price of a booking you have already paid in full.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and debit cards. All accounts must be settled before you leave, unless we have agreed credit terms with your company in writing. VAT receipts are available on request.

7. During your visit

Smoking and vaping

Brandon Hall Hotel and Spa is non-smoking inside, including e-cigarettes and vapes. Please use the outdoor smoking areas. Smoking or vaping indoors may result in a cleaning charge (see section 4).

Behaviour and dress

We want every guest to enjoy their visit. We may refuse entry, refuse service or ask anyone to leave whose behaviour, language or dress may offend or disturb other guests or our team, or who appears to be intoxicated. In that case no refund will be given. Smart casual dress is expected in the restaurant and at evening events.

Food and drink

Food and drink bought outside the hotel may not be consumed in our public areas, function rooms or grounds unless we have agreed this in advance. We follow the licensing laws on serving alcohol and may ask for proof of age.

Leisure and spa

Use of the pool, gym and spa is subject to the rules displayed in those areas, including age restrictions and supervision of children. Spa treatments that are cancelled at short notice, or missed, may be charged in full.

Photography and filming

Photography for personal use is welcome. Commercial photography or filming, including for weddings not held at the hotel, needs our permission in advance.

Your belongings and vehicles

Please keep valuables in your room safe or ask at reception. We are not responsible for loss of or damage to guests' belongings or vehicles, except where this is caused by our negligence. Cars are parked at the owner's risk. Lost property is kept for a limited time, and we can post items back to you at your cost.

Events outside our control

We are not liable if we can't provide a booking, a facility or a service because of events outside our reasonable control, such as fire, flood, severe weather, loss of utilities, illness outbreaks, government restrictions or industrial action. We'll tell you as soon as we can and offer another date or a refund for services we can't provide.

Limits

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law. It does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer.

Competitions
